📖 The Four Laws of Behavior Change

1st Law: Make It Obvious

Design your environment to make good habits obvious and bad habits invisible.

  • Place items in visible locations
  • Set out materials the night before
  • Use visual cues and reminders
  • Create obvious triggers
  • Design environment for success

2nd Law: Make It Attractive

Bundle habits you need to do with habits you want to do.

  • Pair habit with something enjoyable
  • Join a culture where desired behavior is normal
  • Create a motivation ritual
  • Reframe mindset to highlight benefits
  • Make the habit appealing

3rd Law: Make It Easy (2-Minute Rule)

When you start a new habit, it should take less than two minutes to do.

  • Reduce friction for good habits
  • Prepare environment to make future actions easier
  • Master the habit of showing up
  • Standardize before you optimize
  • Start with the gateway habit

4th Law: Make It Satisfying

What is immediately rewarded is repeated. What is immediately punished is avoided.

  • Use immediate rewards
  • Track your habit streak
  • Never miss twice
  • Celebrate small wins
  • Make the ending of your habit satisfying
